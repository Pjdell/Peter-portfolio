15. Your project descriptions currently sound slightly AI-generated

For example:

“Designed to efficiently assist projects based on workload, availability...”

That kind of sentence is polished but generic.

Try writing descriptions in first person or concrete language.

Instead of:

Designed to efficiently allocate resources based on workload and availability.

Use:

I built a Laravel application that assigns faculty based on skills, availability, and current workload.

Much stronger.

Even better:

The idea came from a simple problem: assigning teaching loads manually becomes difficult when availability, skills, and workload all have to be considered at once.

That's human context.
